# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Special:** Children of the Light, I take this body for but a

moment to invoke upon you my blessings and the

blessings of our Brotherhood.

May each of you in your sojourn through this life

accomplish that desire which is uppermost in your

consciousness. May each receive his good or hers, as

they have given.

Great need exists today among your peoples and each

of you have been given the opportunity to serve your

humanity. Fulfill your duties to the very best of your

ability. Give all that you have in the name of Truth and

your reward shall be great, not in the things as the

world giveth, but in understanding, in knowledge and

wisdom will you be rewarded and this, my friends, this

my friends, is this not what you seek, is it not?

May the blessings, the love, the understanding of the

great Brotherhood of Mankind be invoked upon each of

you now.

So be it. Go in peace, beloved. ▯
