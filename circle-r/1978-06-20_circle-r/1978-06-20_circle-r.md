# Joseph

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Joseph:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Joseph.

Again I shall be brief.

The instructions that you have just read may seem to

some to be extremely simple. Some may even despise

them as being of little value in helping or guiding

people.

In the complexity of your civilization those who think

thus forget that the very essence in the life of a pupil is

to be able to lay aside these complexities, to come out

of your world into ours, a world of thought, where life is

simple and direct, where the definition of right and

wrong is clearly defined, where the issues are clear

and to the point.

It is the simplicity that the pupil attains that enables him

to attain the higher positions.

You have made your lives complex, a mass of

confusion, a mass of crosscurrents in which the weak

fall and only the strong survive.

If the pupil is to attain the attention of a Master, he

must take his life in hand, he must wipe aside in his

mind all of these complexities. For unless you become

as a little child you cannot enter the Kingdom of

Heaven, and remember the Kingdom of Heaven is the

Great Brotherhood of the Adepts.

Think upon what I have said, my children, think well.

I leave you by extending the love, the understanding

and the blessings of all the Great Ones.

May you go in peace and may you find that simplicity of

which we speak.

Peace, peace, peace. ▯
