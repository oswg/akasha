# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

The hour is late. ▯

(Night Class on the etheric plane. For the last three

years the class activity has been stepped up. These

are refresher courses as we are getting farther and

farther into the New Age. Most of the lessons are about

things that we have known and experienced before in

other lifetimes, other civilizations such as Atlantis.

This last class was a very large one, held in a huge hall

with thousands of people in attendance. The lecture

given was about the great urgency for preparation for

the coming times. One should develop their spiritual

strength, their physical and mental strength. This was

stressed very strongly. Also, it was stressed that one

should have emergency supplies on hand because the

time is coming when these things will be very difficult to

obtain.)

**Clyde:** One must be strong mentally and function well

under stress. One must have control of their senses

and not become emotional. One must have control of

their mind and thoughts and remain positive.

The situation in our civilization today is like the days of

the Roman Empire and the same solutions are being

tried to correct it but these solutions will not work. So

we cannot put too much faith in our leaders as they will

not be able to help. So the situations will continue to

grow worse.

Inflation will make food almost unobtainable eventually

… Also, one should have a water purifier as

catastrophes such as earthquakes could contaminate

the water. Also droughts could make water scarce.

It will be wise not to be indebted to anyone.

The people in this class seemed to come from

everywhere as there were many different nationalities

there. I believe they were people who are working for

the good of all and who needed to be instructed for

their work in helping mankind in the coming times and

in helping to establish the New Age.

This was an attempt to awaken them to the urgency of

preparing for the times ahead. Most of them will not

consciously remember this class but at the right time it

will come through the subconscious to conscious

awareness as hunches and impressions that they must

prepare.
