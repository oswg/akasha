**Zalhem:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Zalhem. things which seem to you impossible.

There are many things that you will need to learn, Go in peace, beloved ones, go in peace. May your path

many things you will need to develop and to grow ever brighter as you trod the corridors of time.

overcome—if you are to trod the Path of Liberation. Peace, peace, peace. ▯

One of the major things that you must attain is Humility,

Humbleness. There is no place on the Path for the “big

I” and this is not easy for many, for many have built

their egos high. They like to stand out and be noticed,

to receive recognition and praise for that which they do.

This is somewhat a way of life upon planet Earth.

Many times as you trod the Path you will think you

have been forgotten. You will think that all of your work

has gone unnoticed because you have received no

recognition. That “old devil self” is very difficult to

overcome. He continually rears his head and you must

smite him down.

If you would be drawn to one of the Masters, it will be

very necessary to attain a high degree of Humbleness.

Humility, service, will draw the attention of the Master.

He sees those who are serving with humility, with love

and dedication. His attention is drawn automatically to

these and he will watch them closely. He will know your

every attainment.

So as you move along, cultivate this attribute. Never

complain for lack of recognition. Serve only with the

thought of serving your fellow man, thinking never of

self, but only what you can do for others. To brighten

the life of another should bring to you great pleasure,

great satisfactions although this accomplishment may

not have been noticed by anyone, or at least seemingly

so. So that is what we mean when we say you must

become selfless.

We realize that these things are not easy to

accomplish. It requires great discipline and great effort

on the part of each individual, but in time you will see

the value and reap the rewards and you will wonder

why you have not accomplished these things much

sooner.
