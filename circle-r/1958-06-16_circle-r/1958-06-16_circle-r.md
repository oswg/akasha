# Hatton

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

Odina, beloved ones, Odina. **I am Hatton**. ▯

**Hatton:** I greet you, my beloved friends, in the Love

end Light of the Infinite Creator. **I am Hatton**.

Days, my friends, are rapidly drawing near when each

of you who is going to work for your humanity must

take your place and stand out for all to see. In other

words, the workers must take their stand, must stand

firm upon what they know to be Truth.

Opportunities are increasing, as you are aware of,

opportunities to serve, opportunities to become better

acquainted with other workers from other points. The

importance of your work will depend a great deal upon

your response to these opportunities. You see, this

plan is far-reaching. It covers our entire planet. And in

order for this plan to work as it should, it is necessary

that certain individuals be brought together, to know

one another. For when the time comes, when your

service shall entwine with that of others, you will have

knowledge of them, will know of this work.

Many things that are taking place these days are taking

place for a purpose, and this shall be very apparent to

you in the future. You are finding yourselves urged

inwardly to do certain things, to go certain places. You

find yourself being drawn toward certain new

acquaintances. Be aware of these urges and follow

them. Do not ignore them, for this is also a testing

period. The importance of your work shall depend a

great deal upon your response. Yes, beloved friends,

these are the days when those of Light shall be

gathered together in preparation for their days of great

service. You can be as important in this plan as you

care to make yourself. Be not afraid to entertain

strangers, for you may be amazed in the days to come

when you discover who some of these are.

All of you here have been greatly blessed this night, for

Great Beings have stood among you and have poured

out their Love and Understanding to you. May you have

received this Light through your open heart, through

your own Love, extending forth to give and to receive. I

would add my Love and my Blessings to this, for each

of you is near and dear to me.

May the Love and Understanding of the Infinite One

grow ever stronger in your hearts and in your souls.
