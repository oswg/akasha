# Zalhem

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Zalhem:** May the blessings of the Great Ones be

**Zalhem:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Zalhem. bestowed upon you now, and may you persevere in the

pursuit of whatsoever your goal might be.

I would like to say a few words to you about

perseverance. We have tried to give to you, step by Peace, beloved ones, peace, peace. ▯

step, the information and knowledge that would help

you to grow and evolve in the right direction. All of you

need to realize that you have great potentiality, and to

reach that potentiality you have to work at it, and you

have to work on the constructive side of Nature if you

would grow and develop.

It is so easy to slide back into the old way of doing and

thinking, and viewing things from that angle. The fact

that one doesn’t reach a goal as quickly as he would

like or think that he should, is usually because he or

she does not persist in pursuing that goal. You go

along for awhile and then you begin to ease off, and

when you start to do that you back off more and more

and the first thing that you know you have forgotten the

goal. If it doesn’t manifest quickly you lose your

enthusiasm.

But if you will look about you at the egos who have

made great achievements in whatever field it might be,

it is because they persisted and never for a moment

doubted that they could accomplish their desire. The

degree of concentration in some cases, depended

upon what their goal was.

Many handicapped cases have overcome almost

unbelievable odds and have made great

accomplishments. Many have put all of their efforts into

it and they overcame. So, you can be what you want to

be, but you must persist in pursuing that goal until you

have reached it. The fact that you have many failures

along the way, should not deter you in your pursuit.

One may not acquire their goal in full in one life time,

but if it is important enough to you, you will not forget,

and will pursue it later or you may never stop pursuing

it.

Now, my friends, I must leave you. It has been my

pleasure to spend this time with you. I hope I have

been of some service.

**Questioner:** You have, thank you.
