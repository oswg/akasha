**Joseph:** Greetings to you, beloved ones, I am Joseph. joyous.

While you were listening to the lesson of Go in peace, go in peace. Peace. ▯

Samuel and I passed among you and worked upon

your auras, taking out some of the debris that you pick

up in your daily life, and also imparting to you some

beneficial energies.

Now, I do not have much to say except for a few words

concerning the lesson that you heard. In it was

stressed the words Love, Peace and Joy. These words,

my friends, carry great positive power, and it would be

well to repeat these words quite frequently.

It will help to keep you on the positive side. It is very

easy to get caught up in the negative around you, to

become tense, to worry and fret about things which

usually do not occur anyway.

So, when you find yourself slipping into negativity or

beginning to worry or fret over something that you fear

is going to occur, repeat these words:

I will be Positive. I will be Loving. I will be Peaceful. I

will be Joyful.

And if you view things in a positive frame of mind, you

will find that there are many more things to be joyful

about than there are to be worried about. The more you

can keep your mind channeled in these directions the

more you will attract to you the things that are positive,

loving, peaceful and joyous.

Come to the realization that you are going to attract to

you the conditions that you desire, the conditions that

will bring you peace, love and joy, rather than the

things that will bring to you worry, inharmony and

depression. Lift your sights for you and you alone, can

change your life and become loving, peaceful and

joyous.

It is your duty as a child of the One Great Source, to do

this, for only in this manner will you fulfill this mission

for which you were created.

Think upon these things, beloved ones, think well and

make your choice.
