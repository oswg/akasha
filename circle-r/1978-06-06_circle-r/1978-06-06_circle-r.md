# Samuel

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Samuel:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Samuel. I

shall be brief.

We do not wish to mislead you and perhaps from what

we mentioned previously you might expect something

different than that which we shall be talking about.

We will not be bringing you any great wisdom, or great

teachings of the secret philosophies, or giving you the

secrets of nature. What we shall be talking about, by

and large, is the Brotherhood and how it functions, the

various steps in the Path to adeptship. We will be

talking about what is necessary for you to do in order to

be accepted as a chela. We will be talking about your

responsibilities to the Masters and the Masters

responsibility to you. We will be giving you guidelines to

follow in order to qualify.

The Masters take on a great responsibility when they

choose a chela therefore they must be very careful and

we shall explain why.

Many of the Great Masters are living in physical bodies

upon your planet at this time, but they are living in

remote areas that cannot be easily reached. And from

there they do their work in serving your planet. These

Great Beings have a love and dedication that is beyond

your comprehension at this time.

These are some of the things that man must

accomplish if he is to enter the Kingdom and abide

therein.

I leave you by extending to you our deepest love and

understanding. May each of you move rapidly in the

overcoming that is necessary to reach that which you

aspire to.

Go in peace, beloved ones, go in peace. Peace, peace,

peace. ▯
