# Zalhem

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Zalhem:** Greetings to you, my children. I am Zalhem.

I have this to say to you now. Each of you is a center of

light, a spiritual light and the most important thing that

you can do is to develop this light, that it might shine

brighter and brighter.

There are many ways of doing this and we shall go into

this in depth when all of you are present. But be not

anxious for the morrow, just try to improve your light, to

grow, and to know that you will be taken care of, your

needs will be met. Remember always the great store

house, the river of abundance, never runs dry and

unless you block its flow. It will flow to you in great

abundance. Know this. Follow your intuitive guidance.

Do the things that you know you must do and your cup

will be filled to overflowing. Many times your faith will

be tested, but if you remain firm in your knowing and in

your faith, Deity will satisfy your every need.

Seek not the pleasures and desires of the carnal things

but rather seek the kingdom, and all things shall be

added. And where is the kingdom? That is another

thing we will discuss in greater detail at another time.

Now, as I leave, I extend to each of you my peace, my

love and my blessings. May each of you be peace.

Peace, Peace, Peace. ▯
