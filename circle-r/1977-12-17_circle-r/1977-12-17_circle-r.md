# Joseph

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Joseph:** My beloved children, I am Joseph.

I am only going to take a very few moments of your

time, since it is the time of your year when greetings

and blessings seem to be popular with your people.

We who have been working through Center R, as you

are known to us, and through this instrument, all of us

from our Brotherhood, wish to extend our most sincere

blessings to all of those who have passed in and out of

this Center. And to all of those who have been

associated and to those who are now present, we pour

forth our love and our blessings.

Our love for you all is a love which you not yet know.

Our concern for you is much deeper than you can know

or realize. We want only for you that which is for your

highest good, that which will make you strong in His

love. We attempt in every way we can to help you

attain these goals. We realize that sometimes you may

feel that we have deserted you but we have not. But we

must do that which will make you strong for the path of

Truth requires strong hearts and strong minds.

So tonight, we leave our blessings with you and we

extend our love and our Joy to you. May all of you find

great, peace in your hearts, and may this Christ Child

grow to maturity in your hearts.

We wish to extend our gratitude to all of you who have

passed this way, for allowing us to be of some service

to you.

To this instrument, who has given many years of

service by allowing us to use him in this manner, we

extend our gratitude. And to his mate who has served

beside him for these many years, we also extend our

love and our gratitude.

One, whose birth you celebrate will again be returning

to your planet. How soon this will take place will

depend upon the Light workers of planet Earth—how

soon they can prepare the way, for He cannot return

until the way has been made clear.

Peace, my beloved friends, peace. Go in peace and

may peace be with you. ▯
