# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Clyde:** As we were meditating the room filled with an

orange light, the room became orange. Then a rotation

started in the middle of the room, like a vortex.

It gradually picked up speed until it was turning rather

rapidly in a clockwise direction. It seemed as though it

just picked up that color and wound it into this cone-

shaped vortex.

Then after all this had been wound into the cone, the

room filled with a green color. As the vortex began to

absorb it the orange moved down and the green

formed the top of the vortex.

Then blue began to fill the room, then wound into the

vortex. Then both the green and the orange became

more narrow to make room for the blue.

Yellow then filled the room and went into the vortex.

The yellow took the top position, then came the blue,

green, with the orange on the bottom. These were all

medium range colors.

As the vortex rotated, the colors began to, one at a

time, stream out and sweep the room over those

present. This continued for a short time.

Then the colors started to leave the cone as the speed

of the rotation of the vortex seemed to slow down and

the colors slowly faded away.

I did not see anyone causing this to happen. ▯
