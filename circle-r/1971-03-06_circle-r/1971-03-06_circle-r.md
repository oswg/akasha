# Nathaniel

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Nathaniel:** I greet you, my beloved children, I greet you

in peace and in love.

I came not to bring to you scores of words, but I came

rather to bring to each of you a special blessing which

we hope will be of great benefit to each of you. Now I

am going to leave this instrument in a few minutes and

I am going to stand before each of you and I shall pour

out a blessing upon each of you, reinforced by other

Masters and we hope it shall be beneficial in many

ways to each of you.

We would ask that you make yourself comfortable.

Relax, take on an attitude of praise and thanksgiving.

Let each of you pour forth from your innermost self the

praise that you feel for your Creator. Let peace and joy

overtake you. Open your hearts to Him and let this

great river of life-giving energies flow into your hearts

and let it give you peace, joy and understanding.

Peace, peace now reigns in the hearts of each of you.

You are each a channel of love and peace.

I come to you, one by one.

(Pause while this is done.)

It is done. It is our most sincere wish that you have

received as we have given unto you.

May each of your hearts be raised to new heights in

His Glory. The “Day of the Lord” is not far off. Heed His

teachings. Be observant of His ways and follow Him.

Use his laws in everything that you think, that you wish,

that you do, that you say, for He is the Light and the

Way. Let His Way be made clear.

The Father hath need of the talents of all men. Each

man is endowed with God-given talents and they were

given to him that he might use them to glorify God. The

Masters have need of your talents.

I go and you may follow.

Peace be with you, beloved friends. May His love and

His peace dwell ever in your hearts.

**I am Nathaniel**. ▯
