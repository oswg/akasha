**Nathaniel:** Greetings to you, beloved ones, I am

Nathaniel. I am going to take a moment of your time.

Zalhem and I have been here for quite some time. We

have been working with each one of you, cleansing

your auras, and giving you other energy treatments.

Zalhem, before he departed, asked me to say a few

words to explain why we have been so long here. I

know you were becoming a little impatient but this does

take some time, you know.

We also know that you enjoy having Zalhem or one of

us do this for you personally through the boy here, but

that also requires much more time when there are

several of you to work on.

**Questioner:** We appreciate this very much.

**Nathaniel:** So with two of us working this way we can

work a little faster and really do a better job than we

would be able to do the other way.

And that is why it has taken so long. I know that you

were getting somewhat impatient and wondering what

was going on.

So now that you all have had your treatments, we hope

you will feel more invigorated and more peaceful and

joyous, so I will not take more of your time.

**Questioner:** Thank you very much for all that you do

for us.

**Nathaniel:** I give you each my blessings, my love, my

understanding.

I invoke the blessings of the Great Ones upon each of

you.

Go in peace, beloved ones, walk in peace. Be

peaceful, be loving, be joyous.
