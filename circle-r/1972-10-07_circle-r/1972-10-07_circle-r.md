# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Samuel:** Greetings to you, my beloved friends. I am

Samuel.

There has been a concentration of cosmic, healing

energy concentrated here for you this night. We would

like you to use this energy. We would like you to learn

to use this energy, for in times to come this will be very

important to many of you. Now I am sure that most of

you have been aware of this energy tonight, for it has

been concentrated here for some time. Now we should

like each of you to take this energy into your being.

Offer yourself as a channel for this energy. Ask that

you might be filled with it. Take this energy and direct it

to any part of your body that may be in need of healing,

Ask that this energy may bring about in you, harmony

where it is needed. That those parts of your body may

be brought into harmony, into tune with His universal

mind. Direct this energy to every cell of your body. See

it flowing from head to toe, balancing and harmonizing

every cell of your being. See it also flowing through the

soul, through every part, bringing it also into harmony

with your creator. Let an attitude of love, of gratitude, of

praise and thanksgiving flow forth from your being,

knowing that you are because He is, and this being so,

you are made whole.

Now that you are filled to overflowing, direct it to those

whom you would help, to those whom you know who

are in need, and as you send this forth, send it forth in

love.

In the name of the Christ, it is done.

Let us give praise and thanks to the All Knowing One,

We hope that each of you have gained much this night.

We would like to see each of you practice the use of

His healing energies,

Learn to command them, to draw them forth, for as I

have said there will be much need in the days ahead

and we wish to see all light workers prepared to do His

work.

Peace be with each of you, beloved ones,

Peace. Go in peace.

Learn to use the healing and protective energies. ▯
