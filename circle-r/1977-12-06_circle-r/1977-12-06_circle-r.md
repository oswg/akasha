# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Clyde:** A radiant Being, dressed in a purple robe with a

gold sash, appeared.

Our individual Teachers (Wisdors) said, “All hail” to

him.

He then went to each one, and each drank from the

goblet which he offered to them.

The goblet was engraved with the words “Brotherly

Love.”

After each had drunk, he touched them with his two

forefingers on the forehead, the third eye.

Then he said, “May the quaff from the cup enhance

your ability to love one another as we love you.” ▯
