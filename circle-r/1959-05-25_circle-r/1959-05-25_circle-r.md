# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Teacher:** Good evening my friends.

Once again, I shall take the opportunity to say a few

words to you. My friends, each of you must awaken

within you, the realization that now, now is the time, to

bring your being into balance, to weed out of your

consciousness the undesirables, to build upon that

foundation that you have set. For the time draws

nearer, my beloved friends, when in order to

accomplish your aims, your desires, it will be necessary

for you to have control. A new standard of values must

be established. A goal of accomplishments should be

set. A definite something to strive for.

Stay clear of that which tends to pull you down. This

physical structure you now occupy, is a very intricate

piece of mechanism. It is affected by many things. That

which you think, has a great effect upon the

movements of the electrons of this structure. Anger,

hate, greed, and any of these things tend to disrupt and

unbalance the electrons, causing the cells to become

out of balance, and many things may result.

Learn relaxation. Be conscious of what you do, but not

anxious. Anxiety, tension, brings about weakness in

this structure. Learn to close these things out, by

placing about you a wall of resistance. Simplicity, my

friends, simplicity in your life is important. Lead the

simple life, as have all of your Great Masters, for life in

its purest form is simple. So let us feed our body and

souls that which tends to give them strength, which

tends to make them great. These, my friends, are

suggestions that you might be a happier and more

rounded soul. That you might attain greater heights in

your growth and in your understanding. For to grow

and grow properly, one must obey the Law. If one

obeys the Law, the Law will serve you.

Peace be with you my friends. Peace be with you. ▯
