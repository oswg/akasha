# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Clyde:** As we were meditating a familiar but unknown

being came in and led a ceremony of chanting and

symbols. Joseph, Nathaniel, and all of our personal

teachers took part in this.

The blue in the room became very dense. After the

dense blue was established it faded into various

shades of blue and then into shades of green. At one

time there was a mixing of all the colors blending

together like a rainbow. Each color was fading from

dark to light then fading into another color.

When this was well established, Nathaniel took a

golden goblet around to everyone. The goblet had

“Brotherly Love” written on it and resembled one they

used in a ceremony that took place sometime ago.

The ceremony continued, the colors were vibrating and

changing. There were tones that corresponded to the

colors.

After Nathaniel had been to everyone, the colors began

to fade and the tones became less and less. ▯
