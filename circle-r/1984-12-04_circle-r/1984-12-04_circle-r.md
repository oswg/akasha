# Zalhem

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Zalhem:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Zalhem.

**Questioner:** How can you put up with us sad sacks?

**Zalhem:** Well, that’s not easy. It’s a good thing I have a

lot of patience. I don’t know how I’m going to continue

to tolerate you Earth people.

**Questioner:** Please hang in there, like we have to.

**Zalhem:** So, it looks to me, in gazing around here, that

all of you could stand a good cleansing and a color

treatment today. So I will dispense with the words and

Nathaniel and I will get busy and do out work.

**Questioner:** Are you going to leave?

**Zalhem:** Yes, I will leave the boy here and we will work

on you, clean you auras and fill them with some good

vibrations.

**Questioner:** So you are saying good-bye now?

**Zalhem:** Yes, I’m saying bye-bye, but I’m still going to

be here, but not speaking or using the boy’s vehicle.

**Questioner:** We thank you and appreciate all that you

do for us and give to us. And do please come back.

**Zalhem:** Sometime I might, if you are good boys and

girls.

**Questioner:** We are going to really try to be.

**Zalhem:** Be positive, open and receptive to the

energies that we will now give to you.

Peace, beloved ones, Peace. Peace. ▯
