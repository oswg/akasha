# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Clyde:** I saw as well as experienced—I know that I was

present—the following ceremony that took place. It

occurred either in an open place or in a very large

room. But it seemed like it was outside.

Everyone here tonight was present, plus a number of

other people who have been here with us. Also, many

were there with whom we have been closely

associated in the past.

We were standing in a large circle with an opening at

one end where there were two large tables. Each

individual’s Etheric Teacher was there standing by

them. Then the Teachers, in turn, walked to the two

large tables. On the tables were many golden goblets.

Standing by the tables were two beings, one with

whom we had been very close in the past. (Name.) He

was filling the goblets from a gold pitcher, one of

seven, which was labeled “Hope.” Then he handed

them to the other being of great light, who in turn, gave

one to each of the Teachers.

The Teachers then walked to each of their pupils and

stood in front of them. This procedure took place seven

different times. Each time the goblet was offered, it had

a different label or word on it. The words on the

goblets, in the order presented, were:

HOPE - FAITH - HUMILITY - TOLERANCE - LOVE -

WISDOM - POWER

The Teachers were wearing purple ceremonial robes

and each one wore a pendant. Each time they gave a

goblet to their pupils, the Being of great light—at the

tables, who had handed the filled goblets to them—

spoke a few words very fast, in a tongue that I did not

understand. Nor did I have any feeling or impression of

what the words meant.

This was a beautiful, enlightening and inspiring

ceremony. ▯
