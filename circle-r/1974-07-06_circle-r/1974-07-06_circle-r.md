# Joseph

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Joseph:** All hail to you there and to each of you my

special greetings. **I am Joseph**.

I have little to say for I’m going to leave this instrument

again in a moment and I and another will stand before

each of you and pour out a special blessing for each of

you. Now this will take some time so you may as well

discontinue your recording device. I go.

(Pause)

It is done. We hope that in the days to come you will

have received much benefit in many ways.

May each of you go forth this night with greater

confidence, with greater hope, with greater love and

may you carry this with you from now on.

Let the joy and the peace of the Great Ones sing in

your hearts. Let your hearts rejoice and be exceedingly

glad for the days are soon to arrive that will set you

free. Free from more than you think or realize. For

these times will be the removing of the chains and the

shackles from those who walk in the Light.

May His grace and His love shine upon you and upon

your path as you go forth. My love I leave with you.

I go. ▯
