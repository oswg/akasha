# Joseph

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Joseph:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Joseph.

I am only going to take a moment of your time for I

know that your hour is late. I came only to extend to

each of you my blessings.

May the Christ Child be born in the hearts of each of

you and may you nourish this Child to maturity rapidly.

May each of you find great peace in your hearts.

I ask that the blessings, the love, and the peace of all

the Great Ones be bestowed upon each of you.

May each of you in your journey inward find that, that

will bring you that which is for you, which will take you

on the path that will give you that which is needed for

your growth and development.

May you find that peace and joy that you seek. Go in

peace, beloved, go in peace. Peace, peace, peace. ▯
