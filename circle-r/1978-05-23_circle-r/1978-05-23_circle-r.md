# Samuel

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Samuel:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Samuel.

I am only going to say a few words at this time but I

should like to, for a moment, give you some idea of

what we will be doing in the near future.

We will be talking about many things, among them will

be some rules that we wish you to follow and some of

the things that will be necessary for you to accomplish

before you are chosen as a chela.

A few of these things that I might mention will be the

effect upon the individual of anger, resentment, of

laughter and of joy. We shall be talking about children

and some of the things that we might say might be

somewhat difficult for you to accept or understand, yet

as you reach greater heights on the Path, you will

realize the Truth of our words. We will be talking also

about thought and the effects that it can have, both

upon yourself and upon others and here again you may

find some surprises.

But we just want you to know that we are interested in

your progress and that we are going to help you in

every way that we can, to move along the Path. All of

you will need to make some changes and these

changes will not be easy. It is not easy for one to pull

himself from the old ruts and develop new ways and

new procedures. We realize that many of these things

are very deeply seated and will not be rapidly

eliminated from your consciousness and your ways of

life.

Know always that we understand your problems and

that we are with you, supporting you and encouraging

you in every way that we can. But there will be times

when you will doubt this very much.

May each of you be able to keep your courage high

and your intents pure, and that will be all that I have to

say at this time.

May the peace and the love of the Great Ones

surround each of you and may this love penetrate

deeply into your hearts.

Go in peace, beloved ones, go in peace.

Peace, peace, peace. ▯
