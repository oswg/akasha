# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Yaum:** Good evening to you, my friends.

I have a few moments tonight that I would like to spend

with you if you have any questions?

**Questioner:** Would you please comment on

Kohoutek?

**Yaum:** There are many who are baffled because of its

action or non-action, as some of the Hierarchy are

baffled. Things do not always work out in the universe

as they have been calculated to do. Something

happened as the comet went around the sun. It

seemed to have burned out a great deal on its return

trip and lost its brilliancy. Then it did not continue on

the course as had been calculated. This is why you

have had trouble seeing it.

There is a study going on now with the Hierarchy,

trying to determine exactly what happened. There may

have been an intervention by higher powers or perhaps

by the Power of Powers for some reason unknown to

us. This remains to be seen or discovered.

That is all that I can tell you about it as astronomy and

astrology are not my fields. I do not claim to be all wise

but this is what I have been told. So you are not the

only ones who are baffled.

Those from higher realms who are attempting to help

Earth people have not reached the ultimate. They are

not all wise nor infallible. There is much to learn in this

vast sea of life on one’s climb to the ultimate.

Knowledge and understanding comes in degrees and it

is shared with you as it is received, and when you are

ready to receive it. But I am sure that more information

and enlightenment shall be coming to you in due time.

It is again my joy to spend a few moments with you. I

ask that all of the Great Ones pour forth their love and

their blessings upon each of you.

Peace, beloved ones, Peace, Peace. ▯
