# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Clyde:** I see a light over the heads of each of you. Now

these lights are gradually taking the shape of a round

ball which is a silver color, about the size of a

basketball. They are about 3 and a half to 4 feet above

your heads, at identical heights … Now a silver circle

with a cross within is forming on each of the balls as

though they were engraved on it. They are starting to

descend over your heads very, very slowly, until they

are about a half inch above you. They are setting still

now. (This lasted two or three minutes.) They are

starting to ascend very slowly, to their original position

and are now disappearing simultaneously.

There is still a hazy light above each of you and

something else is now forming but now each one is

different. Over H is a triangle, over JE is a circle, over

JH is a square, setting on one corner. These are gold

in color and they are lines, not solid, about one inch

width and one foot in size. They are now descending

as did the first ones until they are a half inch from your

heads. They now have a fluorescent glow around them

which appears to be emanating from them. They are

ascending again to the first position and beginning to

fade.

Something else is now forming. It is a long and short

armed gold cross which is beginning to descend. Now

it is one half inch above your heads, pausing for a few

moments, then ascending to the first position and now

fading out. Now they are gone.

Each of your Teachers is holding their hands above

your heads and saying something that I cannot hear.

Now they have touched your forehead, the solar plexus

and both shoulders, which makes a cross and now they

are gone. ▯
