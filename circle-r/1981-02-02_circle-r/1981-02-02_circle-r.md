# Zalhem

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Zalhem:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Zalhem.

Tonight Joseph and I are going to pass among you and

do some work for you. We hope that each of you will

benefit. So now we shall prepare to carry this out.

I shall move around you anticlockwise and my brother

here will move clockwise.

Now I would like each of you to become completely

relaxed. Do not focus your attention upon us and our

movements, but allow yourselves to become

completely relaxed.

Think of yourself as you were when you were a small

child relaxing in the arms of your mother, with her love

enfolding you.

Let yourself completely relax—every muscle of your

body.

Lay aside all of your tensions and cares, for they are

not real. Allow the God-part of you to stand forth.

Now we shall proceed.

(Long pause.)

It is done. Beloved ones, go in peace. Peace, peace. ▯
