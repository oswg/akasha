# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Teacher:** Beloved ones, peace be with you. There is

about to take place upon your planet, a great

celebration. Much emphasis will be given to peace on

earth and good will toward men. This has gone on for

many hundreds of years. Much talk, much singing,

much preaching, but when we look close at planet

Earth, we find so little of this.

Instead, we see much hatred, much selfishness. How

can people talk such a story and live it so little? But, the

harvest is being reaped and soon this shall end. The

coronation, beloved ones, is at hand. The change to

real peace on earth has begun. The cleansing, the

washing away of all this is at hand. There are many

events to take place before the return, but, the return is

not far hence, when again in your skies will be seen

that great light that shall usher in the new age, where

peace on earth and good will to man shall flourish in its

fullness and all men shall know love and have peace in

their hearts.

Yes, the coronation is at hand, and you shall be there,

shall witness these great events. Give yourself to his

service and really live this peace and good will. Take it

into your heart that your light might shine for all to see.

Come unto me as you are and I will give you Peace.

Peace, Beloved Ones. ▯
