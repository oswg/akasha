# Unknown Contact

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

**Oxal:** Know and understand that creative spirit which

dwells within you, of which you are a part, of which you

are. When you know this, my dear friends, I repeat,

when you know this, then many, many things shall

become clear to you. But that is the first thing that you

must know—that you are—you are—because he is.

Realize at this moment your a oneness with your

creator. We do not bow our head when we worship. We

look up and out. Realize that Christ light within you.

Realize that you are God. That you are a part of that

one, and that you are expressing for him. He is

expressing through you. You are his expression. Once

these realizations are firm in your mind, once you know

who you are, then, my friends, truth shall unveil itself to

you and all shall become clear.

Keep your sights high, my beloved. Demand only that

which is of the highest because you are of the highest.

You and your creator are one. You are a creator. You

are a co-creator with the one great all powerful creator

of all. Realize this. When this realization of your a

oneness with the father becomes a part of your

consciousness, you shall find a whole new universe

opening to you. Declare your rightful place, your son-

ship with your creator. Learn to look out from your

creator, rather than up to. You of yourself, I of myself,

can do nothing, but the father within me and the father

within you can accomplish all things through me, his

perfect expression. All things are mine, all things are

yours, because he is, We are. ▯
