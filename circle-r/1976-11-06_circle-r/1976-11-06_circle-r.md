# Samuel

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

May the blessings and peace of all the Great Ones be

**Samuel:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Samuel. invoked upon each of you now.

I will spend a few moments with you tonight as there Go in peace, beloved friends, go in peace, ▯

are a few things that I would like to bring to your

attention.

I know that too many it is quite discouraging when you

look at the overall picture of overcoming all of the tests

on the path of liberation. But we do not wish to lead

anyone to something unless they are aware of what

they are being led to.

Now you understand that the point in time at which you

stand now you are being given the opportunity to

prepare for the path of liberation and the first steps of

initiation. In looking from where you stand now perhaps

it looks impossible, but you must realize that when you

have reached that point of entering you will have done

much overcoming and will be a much stronger

individual.

Although the path of liberation may still look very harsh

and difficult, if you have the sincere desire of liberation,

you will have the courage to take the step. At that point

you will not be urged or pressured in any way. It will be

entirely your decision as to whether you want to

proceed from that point.

So perhaps sometimes we try to cross our bridges long

before we approach them.

So I say to you, if you are sincere and wish to be

liberated from the false, dark surroundings that you

now find yourself in, be of good cheer and know that

the time has come and that you will have the strength

and the courage to proceed step by step.

And if you continually keep your trust in the Great Spirit

of Creation, that one and only Power that sustains all

things and all life, you will emerge triumphant and the

rewards, you will find, will be worth all of the efforts, all

of the trials and much more. You will be happy and

elated that you took the step—and as you look back

you will say, “It was worth every step that I took and

much more.”

May each of you find courage, strength and wisdom.

May your progress go well.
