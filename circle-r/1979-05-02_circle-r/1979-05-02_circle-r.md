# Raphael

_(Clyde Trepanier channeling)_

(Not recorded. Compiled from group notes.)

**Raphael:** Greetings to you, my children, I am Raphael.

I was asked to come here tonight to speak to you about

the reason we contacted the instrument in the manner

that we did. I wish I could meet all of you here but this

is not possible or needed. We contacted the instrument

to show you and help you realize the closeness of the

Brotherhood to you. You, many times, feel these

teachers of the Brotherhood are way out in another

dimension and it seems difficult to relate to the

Brotherhood but I assure you, we are here working

closely with you.

Two incarnations ago I set my foot upon the Path.

There were times when I felt like turning back,

however, the Joys attained were worth the sufferings

experienced.

This is my last incarnation here that I am required to

fulfill for I am finishing up some karma while also

working with the Brotherhood. Now I may incarnate

back here again but this would be my choice.

I cannot reveal my work with the Brotherhood for this

would only hamper it. As I work with the Brotherhood,

however, I do to the best of my ability what I am asked

to do, for you cannot be good at giving orders if you

cannot follow orders given to you. This is an important

lesson to learn in the Brotherhood.

I must work for my livelihood and also pay my taxes

like you do, however this is not my most important

work.

There will be trials in the times to come so we must be

strong souls to be able to stand in the fire. We must

master our lower selves and our emotions. We must

strengthen ourselves morally and physically, especially

the younger ones, in order to survive the coming times,

for the material things cannot be relied upon. They can

change and disappear quickly. The only thing you can

rely upon is your spiritual strength as this can never be

lost or taken away from you.

It has been my privilege to spend this time with you and

as I leave I give to each of you my blessings.

Go in peace, peace, peace. ▯
