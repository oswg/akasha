We are Q'uo. We are now with this instrument. We are pleased to again be with this circle. The beauty of your collective light and collective seeking is beyond words. We begin with our customary reminder for all who may hear or read these words. Use careful discernment that anything that does not resonate as true be cast aside and not be given further thought. The topic we are called to speak on today is that of the journey of self-discovery. That from the perspective of the third density entity seems apparently obvious yet cloaked in mystery. As although we may be entities of vast experience and multitudes of incarnations, that we undergo forgiveness so that we may proceed through this incarnation from a clean slate. The so-called self-discovery may be better understood as remembering. We have, through the careful yet subtle guidance of our higher selves, set up certain signposts, fail-safes, that when our seeking is attuned or the need is great and the calling is sincere, may be revealed to us along the way. The most readily available and commonly dismissed method of contact with the so-called unconscious mind is the feature or tool known as dreams. A method cherished and pursued by this instrument. Every night as we sleep, we enter a liminal space of a character very different from the conscious mind. And are able to experience catalysis, lessons, both potent and mundane. Through which only a concentrated effort are we able to recall and work with. One of the most important modes of interpretation of the unconscious mind through these messages is honesty. As with all movement from catalyst to experience, the jewels are fascinated through only the most rigid honesty and also care. In your world of dazzling distractions, forceful personalities, glittering technology, there are so many frameworks and models we can apply to ourselves. We are offered so much. It's so simple to simply adopt an appealing image or collect witty phrases and cherry pick personalities and apply them to ourselves. As your friends, you have your work cut out for you. And finally, as we have so many other messages, I would again remind you that only through meditation and honest review may the process of self-discovery be made more clear and more potent. And with this, we would like to express our gratitude for inviting us to your circle. We are so pleased and wish to extend our support and love to all and especially these new instruments. We now take our leave of this instrument and leave you in love and light. And remember, we are cool.
