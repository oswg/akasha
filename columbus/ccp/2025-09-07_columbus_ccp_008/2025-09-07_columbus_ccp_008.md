# Group question

How can we work with the 4th density incoming energies to achieve more focus during the changes in our lives?

# Channeled message

_(Jeremy channeling)_

**We are Q'uo.** We are with this instrument and greet you all in the love and light of our One Infinite Creator. Our most humble blessings we offer as your brothers and sisters of the Confederation of Planets in the Service of the One Infinite Creator. This is our first contact with this circle. As such, we offer you our invitation to the tradition of interplanetary working that we symbolize, for are we not a principle composed of many of those whose names you have heard previously in this circle? Therefore, rejoice my brothers and sisters: the time is at hand when many centers of light will blossom to guide your people through a very dark and murky time, a time that will offer many challenges. However, it is challenge that promotes growth, and what is before you is not distant. We speak of your coming harvest into those energies which your question addresses this evening. 

It is incumbent upon us to offer our standard caveat. We will speak of the trials and tribulations of a life lived as a testament to the unity of all, and this majesty percolates into your illusion in ways that often do not seem to be what you expect. This is the hallmark of the new, the calling card of the other side of your beingness. Shall you greet it as such? My brothers and sisters, that decision is yours. 

You see, we offer much love, much information, and much of a conditioning vibration that may, in our estimation, assist you on your path. But is it _our_ path, my brothers and sisters? In some ways, yes; for we are, after all, one. However, because we are one, each portion of the one is free and at complete liberty to express its will, its distortions, its signature in the overall band of vibration that this octave comprises. Should you feel any trepidation in joining us on this path, is there any danger at all in stepping aside and giving it a second thought? No, my brothers and sisters: you cannot err. 

We simply encourage you towards that which approaches the optimum of seeking efficiency. And given this—-given the stakes, both exhaustive and trivial--the matter must be left to you: each of you. A conditioning wave is not a validation of your thinking. A reaching out from our principle is only a bid, my brothers and sisters, a kind of offering. If it cannot be rejected, it cannot be freely accepted. So choose for yourselves. Choose at the time of your choosing, and let your hearts open at this moment, considering the full measure of our message at perhaps some moment when you have your full wits about you. Do us this favor, and we will be ever so grateful. For our mission is to help, and even from our vantage point it is not always clear how this may be accomplished. So your help, simply by being who you are, does us so much honor it is difficult to convey, and we are in your debt. 

As we continue our message to examine the nuances of the question which your circle has deeply considered, we feel that the focus you have each achieved in this working might be reflected upon. Entities such as yourselves feel most alive when the full energies and potential within your complex have been released, allowed to transition into a kinetic state, and the feedback of a reaction to this action fully registering one's bodily presence in the moment. This is the vibrancy and connection that certifies to the seeker that, whether it is danger ahead or glory, it is real, it is yours, and it is investing this experience with the meaning you crave. 

But this is not every moment, is it, my brothers and sisters? What perhaps is the meaning, the value of the dilated focus, the mind wandering, the languid posture of the body, and the steady-state hum of the complex of spirit continually radiating regardless of one's exercise of this faculty of connection? There is hot and cold, night and day: opposites abound in your experience, and balance is an ideal that one strives towards. In the phenomenal unfolding of events, each of which promises the mind/body/spirit complex proximate to it an avenue of expenditure of its love, its love, its light, its power, this balance has no leverage, no asymmetry that creates the propulsion towards the goal. 

The goal is, of course, quite illusory, but do you not _crave_ this illusion? Is it not in your bones, your DNA, your limbic brai to extend further, to exhaust yourself in the striving for this? This is the intelligence of your body, my brothers and sisters. You do not need to cultivate this, per se; it is built in. It is built in because what we have just described, this imbalance, is the nature of manifestation, the nature of that portion of the illusion which those of our kind have named space/time. There is completion and resolution at the level of the One Infinite Creator, but you are created to experience the imbalance so that the composite features of this Creator may be brought out into distinction so that they may achieve this focus. And therefore, when you are unfocused, you have created conditions where you may strive towards a concentration of your attention, your awareness, your energies that lead to the next unfolding of the mystery.

Do not fret about a lack of focus. Be where you are at, we would advise. This is good and an opportunity to exercise faith so that you may learn how to be all of this creation as your bodily investment in manifestation and not just the parts that validate you, that you may ascribe to your character, your accomplishments. For these items, these medals you pin on your lapel, they will dissolve so quickly, my brothers and sisters. You know this well. 

The steady state you bring from your deep mind--from that mind that abuts time/space--is the exercise of your balance in the moment, in the tumbling of moments into fruition. And it is all the more poignantly done at the dusk of third density when mind/body/spirit complexes such as yourselves try to, shall we say, cram in as much learning as possible. Again, you need not force this to happen, but it is understandable that you might strategize so. 

To speak more on how these energies might be cooperated with--even utilized-- we would offer the one known as Ben our contact. My brother, we advise you that our angle of entry will be at the so-called third eye. He will feel us as a pressure that will disclose the first words of our message. We ask you to take the appropriate time to steady these energies and then to speak the words that this pressure engraves upon your soul, and in this way, we may have communion.
