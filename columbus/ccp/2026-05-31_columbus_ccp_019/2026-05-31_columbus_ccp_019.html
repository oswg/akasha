Session 19 - 5/31/26 Q’uo on Love & Suffering

(Ben channeling)

We are known to this circle as Q’uo, and we are with this instrument. We greet you all in the love and the light of the one infinite Creator. As this circle has come to expect, we will dispense with our usual caveat. It is of the utmost importance that anything that we communicate to this circle tonight not be held above any other words or thoughts which you may hear from a friend. We do not wish to be held at a greater status than your own, but viewed simply as a friend who has had slightly more experience than yourselves have had, in your current incarnation. It is therefore very important that what we say tonight is free to be accepted or rejected.

The simplest method of doing this is by following the path of resonance. Take and accept what resonates with you and your being, and discard the rest. As we have said, this is central to our own service, which we offer to you now. We live in a creation in which free will is the utmost authority. At every section of creation one finds complete and total free will and therefore free will must be respected above all else. This is why we have taken great lengths to speak to you in this manner, and why we insist that you have the option of deciding for yourself. With that being said, we will proceed.

We join this circle tonight and share our thoughts on love and some of the deeper meanings of love and life in general. We would start by saying that we often have a simple message. There are those who may come expecting great secrets of the universe to be revealed, but alas, there are no great secrets. The truth is already known, deep inside each of your beings, waiting for the time and the place in which each of you use silence to uncover it and reveal it, from self to self. The secrets are kept only within. We usually offer a simple message, which we hope serves as a reminder to look within and find the truth that surely lies deep inside.

The last time that we spoke to your circle, we spoke of the walls that each of you have built tirelessly and with great effort around each of your hearts since you were a child. It is those great walls that cause you the pain and the difficulties that you experience in this life. Suffering is, in fact, a choice. As you go through your life, you may discover other people living in conditions that are unimaginable, what you would call deplorable. This is your own term, not ours.

However, you may find some people in these conditions who are immensely happy and are able to find joy in situations that would appear to contain no joy at all. And yet the question must be asked, how are they able to do this when your own life is oftentimes one of complete luxury, and yet every little thing that you experience is experienced as suffering.

There is so much suffering, as far as the eye can see, and yet, we say to you, suffering is a choice. Whether or not you believe it or you accept it. It is true. It can take a lifetime (to understand this). It is, in fact, the great work of a lifetime to begin to demolish these walls that you have built around your heart since you were a child. They were put in place by your parents, the authorities around you, your culture, and most of all yourself. It is these walls that prevent the free flowing love from within and from those around you.

It is not until one sets their sights on these walls, understands that they exist, that they may in fact begin to take them down. As the walls are taken down, brick by brick, it opens the door to an unspeakable joy, a love for yourself and a love for those around you. As we have said before, you came into this world bright eyed and full of love, ready and willing to share it with all who would accept it and be a radiant little person. You have spent a lifetime closing yourself off, closing yourself off to this innate desire. We do not blame you, for we have been in your position before many times, and we understand the difficulties. In fact we have observed your planet for a very long time, and we will say that even among the cosmos, the complexities of the cultures in which you currently occupy are quite extreme. It is rare that life becomes as complex and as difficult as the lives that you currently live now. This may be hard to believe, but we simply share our own experience with you.

One of the most difficult parts of disassembling these great walls is when the wall is taken down enough, and one is able to catch a glimpse, a glimpse of your own heart, and suddenly so much pain and distress and agony gushes out. You compulsively reach for that brick that you just removed, and you put it back in its place. Acceptance of oneself is far more difficult than acceptance of those around you. We have spoken on this before, but we would say that the journey of accepting oneself will surely be longer than the journey of accepting others. You have undoubtedly noticed this as you’ve gone about your life.

There is so much to be said about this and we can only encourage you, begin the process of viewing yourself, understanding yourself, accepting yourselves, loving yourselves. It is this process that is in fact the great work in this life, for you cannot truly love someone else until you love yourself. For is it not true that we are all one? Some of you may not acknowledge this in your rational mind, but if you look deep enough in a moment of silence, this truth may surface. It is this oneness, this great unity of creation in which we have come here to share, for it is not well known among your peoples that this creation is built on unity.

We are all, in fact, a single entity. A single entity who became aware of itself and had a desire to understand itself, and within this desire the creation was formed, and here we find ourselves currently understanding ourselves. You have come into this life knowing nothing, and yet as you brush the dirt away, you may find little glimmers of gemstones waiting for you to dig them out and inspect them. The more you dig, the more you will find, and the more you will understand yourself. There is so much mystery, and yet it does not matter where you came from and where you are going. It simply matters that you are here now, and you have come here with the intent of understanding and accepting yourself, and as a result of this process, loving yourself and each other. This is the simple truth that we sought to share tonight.

There is no greater mission or goal in this life than to love yourself and love one another. We understand how difficult this can be. We see the weight you carry in your hearts and in your beings, and the suffering that plagues you. And yet, as each of you have noted tonight, there is so much joy that can be experienced when you simply come together and revel in the love that you have for one another. There is so much truth in these experiences. We encourage you to ask one another, why does it feel so good to love one another, to look into the eyes of another person, and see them radiating the love back into your own eyes, in which you are expressing to them. When you do this, you catch a glimpse of each other’s souls, and if, but for a moment, you remember, and you feel the unity that exists, that this universe, this creation is built on. Love is, in a manner of speaking, an expression of this unity.

As always, we would, but take a moment to remind the circle that the most efficient and effective way to begin dismantling these walls around your heart, and beginning to understand and accept yourselves is by developing or cultivating a love for the practice of what you call meditation. We do not offer much in the way of guidance, and we simply encourage that you chart your own path. Meditation is one of the reasons that we are here and have been here speaking to your circle and others around this planet, for we come offering a simple message of love and of silence. Existence on this planet was established with meditation as a resource that is available to you, and it is the most useful tool that can be implemented into one’s own life. We feel that there is a lot more that could be said, but we have recently decided to offer more abbreviated messages to your circle, so that we do not utilize too much of the energies that are offered. We would remind the circle that we are the principal known to you as Q’uo, and we would now like to open the floor to questions.

Eric: I have a question. Is it possible that any of the walls that have been built should stay in place, and if so, how do we discern between the walls that should be taken down and the ones that should stay in place?

We are Q’uo, and we are once again with this instrument. We understand your query, my brother. We will start by acknowledging the position or the place in which you come from. The dismantling of these walls can be a frightening undertaking. You have spent a lifetime building them, ensuring that they are secure, even polishing them, and in most cases they are quite solid. A better analogy, perhaps, might be that there is a lock or cage around your heart, a lock of a very intricate nature. As you begin this process, it may take you a very long time to discover the key, or in a simple, brilliant moment, you may reach down, see it shining in the sand, collect it, and open the door to your heart in one fell swoop. It is the goal to open the heart completely. Without your knowledge, it is what you came here to do.

This life that you live, the experiences that you have, the people that you meet, the suffering that you feel, the love that you feel– the ultimate hope that you had before you came here, when you were a soul and you chose to incarnate in the physical vehicle in which you currently enjoy was that you would understand this truth, you would know it, you would feel it, and you would live it. We say to you, we say to all, at the end, if you have reached your goal, the walls will be completely dismantled, every door will be opened, the locks will be removed, and you will be the most brilliant, beaming source of love that anyone has ever seen. This is the goal, albeit it is rarely achieved, and it does not matter much, for as we have said, you have an infinite number of lifetimes to understand and achieve this goal.

With that being said, there is a parable that is written in your holy works of a story of those who have come to work on a master’s vineyard. Those who arrive early in the morning are offered a wage to be paid at the end of the day. As the day progresses, there are those that arrive in the mid afternoon. They begin their work, and yet they are promised the same wage as those who begin in the morning. And then, as the day progresses further and the sun gets lower in the sky, there are those who arrive in the evening, and yet the master promises them the same wage as the first two groups, and when the sun sets and the work is complete, all are paid the same wage. This is the life that you live. This parable speaks to that.

For there are those who may find this truth, and know it, and begin to live a life of love, and they may live their entire life with this knowledge, and they may be that beaming source of light and love in which we speak, and yet there can be those that in the last moments of their life they understand this truth, and they see it, and the walls come tumbling down, and the doors fly open, and as they pass into their next great journey, they have achieved the same result. This is possible because this universe does not contain judgement. There is no judge except for yourselves. It is a simple matter of learning to utilize the love and the light that is available to you.

To return to the question, for we fear that we have gone on a tangent. Every remaining portion of these walls that you have built restrict the ability to harness the love and the light that is available to you. We say to you, say to all, take your time. There is no rush. Stop and smell the roses. Life, what you see around you, is incredible. But if you desire, you can take upon yourself this great work, and you can begin to open the heart, and the speed of this journey is entirely up to you. We are unfortunately not able to offer much in advice beyond the encouragement that you simply use your own discernment. You may find that any uncertainties can be resolved by taking the question into a meditation or a moment of silence, for there are great resources available to all of you, if you only but learn to listen and call on them. We would ask if we have answered your question to your satisfaction my brother?

Eric: Yes. Thank you.

We are known to this circle as Q’uo, we would once again open the floor to any questions that you may have.

Kate: I have a question. You spoke of the complexities of the cultures that we are currently living in. I was wondering if you have any advice on how to navigate through these complexities, or even perhaps work to decomplexify these cultures?

We are Q’uo, and we are once again with this instrument. We understand your query, my sister. As we alluded to, you live in an infinite creation. This is mostly incomprehensible to you in your current state of existence, but from what we have experienced, which is only a tiny sliver of the universe, it is rare that a planet becomes as complex as the one in which you currently reside. We do not mean this in a technological sense, for there are those with technologies far beyond what you utilize, but we mean this in a social sense.

As you are fully aware, the systems in which you must learn and participate in are so overwhelming, and these systems have been established over thousands of years and are effectively immovable. They are largely unchangeable. Those who implemented the systems have worked tirelessly to ensure that absolute control is had. You know this to be true, and thus we are not able to offer advice on an attempt to change the system, but what we can offer is advice that one learns to be unbothered by the system that exists. This can be enormously difficult, for it is clearly seen and understood that these systems cause what you term, enormous suffering. They create great disparities and great disharmony, and yet you cannot change them. We understand the frustration.

When you came to this planet, you knew full well what you were getting yourself into. You could see what lay before you, and you knew the difficulties, and yet you signed up for it. You came here with the hope of being a beacon of love. We encourage a simple learning and simple acceptance of life within said systems, for it is wise to learn to accept that which you cannot change. Maintaining a feeling, an emotion against something that is unchangeable leads to inevitable disharmony. It is this disharmony within yourself that distracts you from the reality and the truth and the joy of one’s own existence. If you look around, you will see so much that you are able to change, and so many people for whom you are able to be that radiant point of light in which you came here to be, and we encourage you, encourage all of you, be that source of radiance, be that source of love.

Do not pay attention to the system that you cannot change, simply participate in it, learn to accept it, and love what you can. We are not telling you to reject it, for you came here to work with it. And if you feel that it is your calling to participate in the system and focus your energies within the system, then we encourage you to do so. For this may, in fact, be your great work. This may, in fact, be your calling. But for the great majority of those who are currently incarnate on this planet, there is so much value in living a simple life, sharing as much love as you can, and radiating joy at every opportunity.

Believe it or not, simply loving and expressing joy can have a much greater impact on the planetary consciousness than striving for a lifetime to change the system. When you step outside and you acknowledge and appreciate the beauty around you, you are having an impact that you cannot see, but is quite visible to us. Each of you is able to offer so much to one another and the planet as a whole by simply learning to accept and love what is. It is circles such as these that have begun the great work of understanding, accepting, and loving one another that have the biggest impacts on this planet. For when you come together and you share that love that you have for one another, you lift the planetary consciousness up. We would ask you, my sister, if we have answered your question to your satisfaction?

Kate: Yes. Thank you.

We would say that the temple of love that your circle has been so tirelessly building has been a beacon of light for those who watch and look for such things. What you offer to one another by simply being yourself is immense and unknowable, but if you simply take a moment to look into each other’s eyes, you might catch a glimpse of what the other feels and knows to be true. We encourage you, do not hesitate to revel in the love for one another, for your peoples almost rarely speak what is on their mind. We encourage you, make a habit to share what is on your mind, for it may have an impact on another that is beyond measure. If you had kept it to yourself, it could be a tragedy. The effect you have on one another is immense. We leave you now in the love and the light of the one infinite Creator. Adonai. ☥
