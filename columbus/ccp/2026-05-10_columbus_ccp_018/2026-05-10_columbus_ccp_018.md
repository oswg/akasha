_(Ben channeling)_

**We are known to this circle as Q'uo** and we are with this instrument. We greet you all in the love and the light of the one Infinite Creator. As you have come to expect, we cannot proceed without offering, first, our usual caveat. Which is to say, it is of the utmost importance that our words are free-- free to be accepted or rejected by each of you. We do not wish to be elevated to a status higher than your own, but instead, we request that you think of us as a friend who has simply come to share some words. It is most important that the option be there, as free will is paramount. Simply accept what resonates and leave the rest behind. With that in mind, we may proceed. 

We have come tonight to offer our form of service in which we engage with your planet and your circle on a regular basis. It brings us great joy to be able to join you tonight and offer a few words with the hope that they may be helpful to you along each of your individual journeys. We would like to speak to you tonight on the value and the importance of what your people refer to as joy. Joy can be something that's difficult to explain, but yet you know it when you feel it, so to speak. 

If you harken back to your earlier years as a child, you spent much time experiencing joy. It was much more common when you were younger, generally speaking. Have you ever stopped and wondered and questioned why your experience as a child felt so different to the one that you are currently having? Why does it seem that so much was lost, so much mystery, so much joy, so much love... 

You may have noticed that children love without question, they offer much to others and yet, as you grew older and you learned the ways of the world and you were steeped in your culture, great walls were assembled around each of your hearts and around your beings. Walls that keep the love in and keep the love from entering. It would seem that most of the years transitioning into adulthood are spent toiling away, assembling these walls and hardening the heart, forgetting about how easy going you were as a child, forgetting about the great mysteries of life, and instead accepting that there are little to no mysteries left. And yet we challenge you, my brothers and sisters, we challenge you to gaze into the deep and realize there is so much mystery! 

The universe is built on mystery. There is so much to be seen, so much to be known, so much to be experienced. And yet, when we watch your people, we notice you spend all of your days, all of the time in each day, worrying and toiling and stressing about the most frivolous things, things that, if you were to die today, would mean nothing to you. We cannot stress this enough, my brothers and sisters, for it may seem that life is crushing you under the weight of concerns and worries and stresses, and yet, we challenge you to consider, does any of this matter? If you travel inwards in a moment of silence, and you consider that question, we suspect you may find the answer to be no. 

We offer this as a simple reminder, a reminder to indulge in the simple things. Enjoy life, learn the lessons that you are here to learn. Take what is offered and offer as much as you can in return, for this is what you came here to do, to learn these lessons, these lessons of love-- love of self and of others, and to offer as much as you can. As most of you know, as we have spoken at length before, there are two paths, and these two paths can each be taken to achieve a union with the Creator. And as we suspect, your circle is proceeding down one path, and therefore we only offer advice which is applicable to this path. 

To return to the original point... If one undertakes this great journey and proceeds down the path of disassembling these walls around your heart, which you have carefully built and have scrutinized, and have made every effort to ensure that there are no gaps in the mortar. If you begin to work down this path of disassembling said walls, you will find that the joy of simple existence comes so easily. For is life not, oh so grand? If you take but a moment to pause and look around and listen to the birds singing. They experience so much joy at their own simple existence. If you could but hear them, the plants express a similar song. They do not worry about what comes next. They simply are, and they simply do what they need to survive and be content with their lives. We encourage you, take a page out of the book of the bird, which you hear singing outside your window at this time. For life really can be that simple. 

We know that we offer what seem to be very simple lessons or advice, but we encourage you to acknowledge that your existence is incredibly complicated on this planet. The things in which it is a necessity for you to spend your time considering and weighing about is very uncommon elsewhere in the cosmos. The systems that have been established on this planet are unbelievably complex, and we say these things to you tonight with the hope that we may be able to plant a seed, a seed that sprouts and that opens the door to a great questioning-- a questioning of the necessity of the time spent, the time spent participating in all the systems that exist. 

This is not to say that you should abandon your lives for you most certainly would benefit more so by not abandoning your lives, but we simply mean to say that life can be simpler than you have all been led to believe. There is so much value in taking the time each day to find a moment of silence and still the mind and reach deep inside and look for that key, the key that you have long since lost-- the key to the doors of your heart. Some may spend a lifetime searching for this key, and others, in a moment of brilliance, may reach down and discover it. Each path, whether it takes a lifetime or it happens right away, is perfect, and there is no shame. There is no shame in any decision that is made. You are here to learn. How best to learn, except by making decisions and making what you call mistakes-- but as we would say, there are no mistakes. 

This concept, this idea, can only be appreciated from our vantage point, a vantage point that you cannot currently appreciate as you are incarnate on this planet, but from our vantage point, we see that you have an unlimited amount of time, an unlimited amount of lives to learn what you desire to learn. Therefore, a lifetime spent filled with distractions, and what your people might term a waste of time is anything but for you really will have as much time as you need to learn. 

We would also say, as you are all slowly learning, that life is oh so grand when you work together, you come together, you find people that you resonate with, and you learn to love each other. It is these lessons of love, this ability you are all identifying and seeing come to the surface, this ability to love unconditionally, without question, without a second thought that you exercise with each other which is oh so valuable. This ability which you foster among this circle can be taken to the outside world and be so useful on your journey through life. 

We say this as a simple reminder, do not hesitate to revel in the love that you have for one another. It is so grand. The brilliance of each of your hearts when you come together is a sight to behold. We have recently decided to make an effort to shorten our messages with this circle, and we feel that this might be a good note to leave on. With that in mind, we would like to open the floor to questions of any kind.

**We are Q'uo**, and we are once again with this instrument. We understand your query my brother and we would say that, as you have come to realize, there can be value in working with any member of the Confederation on a regular basis. We have come to serve, and we are available at any time, especially in meditation. There are certain members of the Confederation, such as the one you refer to as Amira, who have devoted more of their resources to answering the call when it is put forth, and therefore, if you find that you resonate with the one you refer to as Amira, we would encourage you to explore this relationship and make the decision for yourself if what they are able to offer you is of greater value than another entity of the Confederation. We cannot necessarily say whether one is better than the other. For even amongst individuals, the resonance that one feels with one of us can vary wildly. This has much to do with one's own vibration and path. We cannot say much more than this. We would ask if we have answered your query to your satisfaction?

**Jesse**: Yes, thank you. 

**Q'uo**: We would once again ask the circle, if there are any additional questions? 

**Jane**: I have a question, what signs or characteristics could one use to differentiate information that comes in while meditating, information that might be from the higher self or from someone helping, versus random noise that should be ignored?

**We are Q'uo**, and we are once again with this instrument. We do understand your query, my sister. The answer to the question you've asked is not simple, for it can be difficult for one to identify with great certainty, whether words or experiences or a message or feeling that one receives in a meditation is coming from your own imagination or the higher self. There are other sources by which these things can come, other than the higher self, but we will use this as an example for simplicity. 

As we come to you tonight and we offer our message with the caveat that resonance be used, resonance to determine the usefulness of a message. We say, there is great value in learning to utilize resonance in your own personal journey. For the information that we offer you is not altogether very different from the information that may manifest in your mind (during a meditation). For it is not until you have gone down a path that increases one's sensitivity to these types of things, in which you can easily identify the source of the information, but you can never know with absolute certainty. This is by design. For the pursuit-- the spiritual pursuits are built on faith, and this is the aspect that is by design. 

You have come here to forget and to find your path, and this requires faith. When you have an experience in a meditation that offers a meaning to you, we encourage you to assess it, determine how it makes you feel. Consider if you resonate with the experience, and with the possible outcome of the experience if you were to follow the path that is being offered by it. There is no silver bullet, so to speak, for this system of information being passed up through the roots of the mind is intentionally difficult to shine a light on. 

We would point out that one of the benefits of becoming an instrument or practitioner of channeling is that the ability to grasp an incoming stream of information becomes easier as more experience is gained. If one takes a moment to consider why this might be the case, it makes complete sense. For we effectively work through a similar connection as the higher self does to communicate with you. And yet the connection that the higher self uses is protected and no other entity may use it. But as we have said, the streams are similar and there is some benefit. You may find it easier to freely communicate with the higher self if you are familiar with other types of what you call channeling. 

Since this type of communication is extremely uncommon on this planet at this time, the higher self usually has to resort to more convoluted or indirect ways of communicating. Oftentimes signs and synchronicities and small downloads or packets of information are offered into the deep roots of mind and they slowly manifest up to the surface. There are an infinite number of ways in which the higher self may choose to communicate with each of you but the path, the doorway, is most open during a period of silence or what you call meditation. We unfortunately are not able to offer much more in the way of advice other than learn to follow the path of resonance. We would ask if we have answered your question to your satisfaction?

**Jane**: Yes, thank you.

**We are Q'uo**, and we would ask if there are any more questions at this time?

If there are no more questions at this time, we will take our leave. As we said earlier, do not hesitate to revel in the love that you have for one another. You have begun to build, and have been working on for some time, a beautiful temple of love which has become large enough to house this group of friends.
